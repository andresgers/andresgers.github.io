In silico analysis of flavonoids against HSV DNA polymerase UL30

Molecular docking, genomic variation and structural analysis applied to the study of potential antiviral compounds.

About the project

This project integrates bioinformatics, genetic variation analysis and molecular modeling to study the interactions of quercetin and rutin with the DNA polymerase UL30 of herpes simplex virus (HSV). By combining docking simulations with structural and genomic analysis, the work explores the potential of natural flavonoids as candidate antiviral compounds.
